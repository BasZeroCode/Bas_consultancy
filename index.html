COMPANY SETUP

Starting a company in Singapore

  • Business formation
  • Corporate secretarial
  • Administrative services
01

Quick chat

I ask what the business does and who the owners are.

Example: a freelance designer wants to incorporate.
02

Pick structure and name

We choose the structure, usually a private limited company, and check the name.

Example: founders shortlist three names.
03

Gather the details

Directors, shareholders, address, activity and share capital.

Example: overseas founders learn the local director rule.
04

File with ACRA

The QI makes sure the filing meets requirements.

Example: you review it all before it goes in.
05

Set up the basics

Company records ready, plus pointers on bank and accounting.

Example: a startup gets its records in one pack.
06

Stay compliant

Yearly filings and updates, with reminders before deadlines.

Example: a director leaves and it is filed properly.

Compliance work is handled by the company’s Qualified Individual. I don’t give legal advice.
